FreqAlign: Bidirectional Frequency Alignment for Mitigating Contextual Hallucinations in LLM-based Extractive QA

Zeyu Gao, Yizhang Liu*

Knowledge-Based Systems, 2026 First AuthorSCI Q1

A decoding-time calibration method that uses attention-frequency signals to emphasize relevant low-frequency entities and numbers while suppressing unrelated high-frequency tokens. Across six extractive QA benchmarks, it reduces entity replacement and numeric errors by 59% and 66%, with only 3.2% extra inference latency.

DOI

FineForge: Dual-Driven Reinforcement Learning for Fine-Grained Detail Generation in Diffusion Models

Heyuan Gao†, Bangxun Tang†, Zeyu Gao

ACM MM 2026 Oral Second Author

FineForge improves text, logo, and high-frequency detail fidelity in customized diffusion generation through a DIFT-localized edge-aware reward and Advantage-Weighted Local Feature Alignment. Training-time only with zero inference overhead; on DetailBench, it improves OCR accuracy by 14.87 points and reduces DreamSim error by about 32.1%.

FlowOp: Morphology-Agnostic Animation-to-Robot Motion Retargeting via Sceneflow-Conditioned Diffusion

Zeyu Gao, Zeran Su, Peiran Liu, Zicheng Duan, Zelin Tao, Qiang Zhang, Jiahao Ma*

Under review at AAAI 2027 First Author

FlowOp formulates MA-A2R: given an arbitrary animated character video and a target humanoid robot's kinematics, generate executable, physically plausible joint trajectories. Sceneflow-conditioned visual-skeleton encoding, Skeleton-Anchored Attention, and a Rectified-Flow DiT deliver 5.8× lower cross-morphology MPJPE than MoCapAnything on the 7,800+ clip AnimBot benchmark.

Project Page

MedGuardMem: A Verifiable Dual-Memory Framework for Expert-Level Medical Question Answering

Zeyu Gao, Heyuan Gao

Under review at AAAI 2027 First Author

A dual-memory framework that couples K-Memory for SID-based medical knowledge recall with E-Memory for executable TCSA safety constraints. On MedXpertQA-Text, it reaches 27.26% with Qwen3-8B while cutting the contraindication rate from 19.3% to 6.1%, and 43.75% with a 27B model — a new open-source state of the art.

A Review on Automatic Context Engineering

Xinyu Tong, Zeyu Gao, Yixuan Chen, Baosheng Yu, Yibing Zhan, Yongcheng Jing, Dacheng Tao

Under review at EMNLP 2026 Second AuthorSurvey

The first dedicated survey of Automatic Context Engineering, organizing the field into a type-by-step taxonomy: Guiding, Evidence, and Tool context elements across Baseline, Candidate Generation, Scoring, Selection, and Updating stages.
